# Changelog

Format inspired by [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/).
This file serves the repository and the writing of Steam patch notes; RimWorld does not
display it in game.

## [1.0.0] — unreleased

On release: create the `v1.0.0` tag and the matching GitHub release.

First release of the 1.6 port of AmliFurx's **Fox Lamp**, carried to 1.5 by Megapa3yM as
**【1.5】Fox Lamp Forked** and stopped there.

### Added

- **The lantern lights.** `CompProperties_Glower` at radius 10 in the family amber
  `(227,115,32,0)`, `CompProperties_Flickable` for the switch, and
  `CompProperties_Refuelable` on hay, wood or chemfuel — 0.4 a day against a 12-unit frame,
  so thirty days to a tank, and `initialFuelPercent` 1 so a finished piece is lit without
  waiting on a hauler. `destroyOnNoFuel` is false: run dry and it goes dark, never away.
  The colour and the fuel are AmliFurx's own `BambooShootLamp`, the nearest thing he drew
  to this and the same subject. **The radius is not**, and that is rebalancing rather than
  borrowing: his bamboo lamp lights 4.2, the smallest of his five and under everything
  vanilla calls a lamp, and a pool that size would not cover this sprite, which is drawn at
  4.92 cells. 10 is `TorchLamp` and `Brazier`, the fuel-burning lights this piece is a peer
  of, and it falls between his own 8.2 fur lights and the 14.2 of his flagship. The gate on
  this piece is its 24 000 work, not a dim glow.

  **This is the one entry in this file that is not a repair.** He gave the piece no light in
  any version since 2020 and the 1.5 fork added none, and the evidence says that was a
  choice: the four lamps in the required mod all carry a glower, he filed this piece in a
  different architect tab from them, gave it a beauty twenty times theirs, and added a
  glower to another of his own thank-you statues. Even the colour mask that never loaded
  covers the marble shoot and not the lantern — a colour mask, never a glow map. The name
  was the joke, not a promise. It lights here because we wanted it to, which is what makes
  this a Renew rather than a straight port.

  No `CompProperties_HeatPusher`, unlike `BambooShootLamp`: a marble carving warming a room
  is the part that does not read. No `CompProperties_FireOverlay` either — vanilla draws
  that flame at the centre of the cell, and this piece is drawn at 4.92 cells, so it would
  sit at the foot of the sculpture rather than inside the lantern frame.
- `PlaceWorker_GlowRadius`, so the lit radius is drawn while the piece is being placed.
- Support for RimWorld 1.6. Every field of `Fox_Lamp` was checked against the 1.6 game
  files and the 1.6 assembly; nothing in it was broken by 1.6 itself.
- A French translation — the first one this piece has ever had.

### Fixed

- **The colour mask never loaded, in any version, since 2020.** `Graphic_Single.MaskSuffix`
  is `"_m"` and the lookup is `path + MaskSuffix`, so the game asks for `Thankyou/Fox_Lamp_m`
  while the file shipped was `Fox_Lampm.png`. `reportFailure: false` meant it missed in
  silence. The file is removed rather than renamed — see *Notes*.
- **Beauty was never shown in the inspect pane.** The piece is built around a Beauty of 800,
  but inherited plain `Building` from `AF_NaturalBuildingBase` instead of vanilla's
  `Building_Art`, which is the class that appends the Beauty line. `thingClass` is now
  `Building_Art`.
- **The English description was a garbled machine translation.** `不！准！吃！竹！笋！！！！`
  is *"don't you eat the bamboo shoot"*, chopped between characters for emphasis; it read
  *"No! Shoot! Eat! Bamboo! Shoot! ! !"*. It now says what the original says.
- **The description rendered with doubled line breaks.** It mixed real newlines with literal
  `\n` escapes, and `DirectXmlToObject.InnerTextWithReplacedNewlinesOrXML` turns the escapes
  into newlines as well. It now uses newlines only.

### Changed

- A sentence saying what the object actually is now precedes AmliFurx's description, so the
  build menu tooltip describes something. The original joke and signature are kept word for
  word.

### Removed

- **Four of the five buildings, and the architect category.** `AF_theFurLight`,
  `AF_theFurLight_Normal`, `AF_theFurLight_Tiny`, `BambooShootLamp`, the `AF_CITE` category
  and `Patches/Flickable.xml` are all shipped, identically, by 【AF】SydailyFox (Continued)
  — the mod the 1.5 fork declares as its own hard dependency, and which supports 1.6.
  Republishing them would put five duplicate `defName`s against a mod the user is required
  to have. `Fox_Lamp` is the only def the parent mod never had.
- **All twenty-six `Languages/` folders.** `md5sum` over the twenty-six copies of
  `DefInjected/ThingDef/Hello_Lansy.xml` returns **one** hash: every one, French and
  Japanese included, was the same untranslated English stub generated by RimTrans.
- `Textures/Thankyou/Fox_Lampm.png`, 24 KB — the mask that never loaded.
- The `old/` folder, a duplicate copy of the defs for 1.1–1.3, and the `LoadFolders.xml`
  that pointed at it. This build targets 1.6 only.

### Notes

The `defName` `Fox_Lamp` is unchanged on purpose: renaming it would drop the piece out of
any save that already has one standing.

`shaderType` stays `CutoutComplex`, and the mask is removed rather than renamed to
`Fox_Lamp_m.png`. Renaming would hand the shader a mask it has never had, and how that
changes the render cannot be established without launching the game. Removing a file that
provably never loaded cannot change anything.

The numbers are left as AmliFurx wrote them, and they are high: Beauty 800 against 400 for a
vanilla grand sculpture, on a quarter of the footprint, for 80 marble blocks against 400
stuff and 24 000 work against 105 000; `MeditationFocusStrength` 0.4 flat, above the 0.28 a
*legendary* vanilla sculpture reaches. The rule applied is the one from Shallow Water Floor:
correct a number that was never *chosen*, keep one the author wrote down. All of these were
written down, and being out of scale is rather the point of a thank-you piece.

`uiIconPath` and `uiIconScale` are untouched, although the build-menu icon is a *book* rather
than a picture of the marble shoot. It is not a slip: the 1.5 mod's other icons are a sheet of
calligraphy and an acorn on a card. AmliFurx drew a set of stylised cards, not depictions, and
the obvious "fix" — dropping `uiIconPath` to fall back on the building graphic — would have
broken that set.

`Fox_Lamp` carries `CompProperties_Art` without a quality comp, unlike every vanilla art
building. This was verified rather than assumed: `Frame.CompleteConstruction` calls
`CompArt.InitializeArt` itself when the finished thing has no `CompQuality`, and
`CompArt.CanShowArt` returns `true` when `TryGetQuality` fails. The piece gets its generated
title, author and art tab. Left as authored.
